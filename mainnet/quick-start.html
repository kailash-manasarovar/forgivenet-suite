Quick Start

Welcome to forgivenet, the world's first spiritual tech.

This guide will help you make your first forgiveness request on the Ethereum blockchain.

1. Get a wallet

You need an Ethereum-compatible wallet to interact with forgivenet. If you don't already have one, you can get started with any of the following:

  • MetaMask
  • Coinbase Wallet
  • Rabby Wallet
  • Trust Wallet

Install your chosen wallet and follow its instructions to set it up. Keep your recovery phrase private and never share it with anyone.

2. Select Ethereum Mainnet

forgivenet operates on Ethereum Mainnet. Open your wallet and ensure that Ethereum Mainnet is selected before connecting.

Transactions use real ETH. You will need enough ETH to cover both your chosen donation amount and the network transaction fee.

If you would prefer to test forgivenet without spending real ETH, visit the Sepolia test site using the link at the top of this page.

3. Connect your wallet

Return to the forgivenet home page and click Connect Wallet.

Choose your wallet from the available options and approve the connection. Check that your wallet is connected to Ethereum Mainnet before continuing.

4. Submit your forgiveness request

Write your forgiveness request in the text box. Your request must contain between 500 and 2000 characters.

Enter your donation amount in ETH. Please enter more than 0.01 ETH which is the current minimum donation.

When you're ready, click Send Request and review the transaction details in your wallet. Check the donation amount, network and transaction fee carefully before approving.

Once submitted, your transaction can be viewed on the Ethereum blockchain. Your request may also appear in the forgivenet Record.

A final note

Blockchain transactions are public and generally irreversible. Do not include personal information or anything you would not want publicly accessible. Only submit a request and donation if you are comfortable with the transaction.

Thank you for being part of forgivenet.
